How Transit Pass works

The idea

Type a Bluesky handle and get a transit pass for that account: when it joined, how many posts it has made and how fast, how many followers it has, and a route color of its own. Save it, share it, keep it in your wallet.

The three steps

  1. Type a handle. With or without the @, or paste a link to the profile. Any Bluesky handle works, including handles that are domain names.
  2. Print the pass. Choose whether the photo, name and handle show.
  3. Share it. Save or copy the image, or copy the alt text to put on your own post.

What is on the pass

  • Route and line. A number and a line color picked from the account's permanent ID, so the same account always gets the same route.
  • Member since. The date Bluesky has on record for the account, shown in UTC.
  • Pace. Total posts divided by the days since the account joined, so it shows posts a day, a week or a month, whichever reads best. It is an average over the whole life of the account, not a recent rate.
  • Posts, followers and following. The totals Bluesky shows on the profile, as of the moment you print.
  • Pass type. Day Tripper (under a month old), Regular (under a year), Commuter (under three years) or Lifer.

Good to know

  • Public profiles only. If an account has a content label, its photo and name are left off the pass.
  • The whole thing runs in your browser. It asks Bluesky for the public profile and nothing else, and nothing is stored.
  • The numbers keep changing on Bluesky. The pass shows them as of the time printed.
