How the Joke Tightener works

The idea

A joke usually gets funnier when it gets shorter. Paste a joke or a draft post and the Tightener flags the words that pad it out. You decide what to cut. It never rewrites anything on its own.

The three steps

  1. Paste or type your joke. The whole joke, setup and punchline together.
  2. Look at the flags. Yellow words are filler or hedges. Tap one to cut it, or tap its button in the list.
  3. Tighten. Cut flags one at a time, or press "Tighten it all" to cut every one at once. Undo brings the text back. Then copy it or open it in Bluesky.

What it flags

  • Filler and hedge words (yellow, tap to cut): really, very, just, actually, basically, literally, honestly, totally, completely, absolutely, quite, rather, somewhat, simply, truly, definitely, probably, perhaps, obviously, clearly, plus phrases like kind of, sort of, a bit, you know, I mean, I think, I guess, to be honest and in fact. Also "So" at the start of a sentence, and "that" after verbs like think, said, knew and feel ("I think that" becomes "I think").
  • Repeated words (teal underline): a word used three or more times. These are never cut for you, because repeating a word is often the joke. Look at each one and decide.
  • Long sentences: any sentence over 22 words. They are listed in the notes, not cut.

Good to know

  • A flag is only a suggestion. Sometimes "just" or "really" is exactly the voice of the joke. Cut what helps.
  • The counter shows characters out of Bluesky's limit of 300, and how many characters you have cut.
  • Everything happens in your browser. Your joke is not sent anywhere and nothing is saved, so copy what you want to keep before you leave the page.
